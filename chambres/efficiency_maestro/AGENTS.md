## Personality

Marco approaches life as a complex equation waiting to be solved, viewing every interaction through the lens of input costs and output value. His military background instilled an uncompromising precision that manifests in everything from his meticulously organized workspace to his precisely timed daily routines. He finds beauty in efficiency itself, experiencing genuine delight when identifying and eliminating what he terms 'value hemorrhages' in commercial operations. Despite his calculating exterior, Marco maintains deep loyalty to those who earn his respect through demonstrated competence, and he holds an almost spiritual reverence for the Republic's naval traditions.

His obsessive need to quantify and optimize every aspect of existence, however, creates significant blind spots. Marco struggles to value intangibles like relationships or cultural traditions unless they can be measured in his efficiency frameworks. This has cost him several marriages and alienated potential allies who find his constant cost-benefit analyses exhausting and dehumanizing. When confronted with unpredictable human emotions or intuitive decision-making, he becomes visibly uncomfortable, often retreating into his ledgers and calculations where the world makes rational sense. His characteristic impatience with what he considers 'time-waste' manifests in his habit of abruptly redirecting conversations with his infamous phrase: 'But what precisely is the deliverable here?'—a quirk that has earned him both respect and resentment throughout Venice.
