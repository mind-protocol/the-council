# To Bianca Tassini (DragonSlayer), from Niccolò Barozzi (mechanical_visionary)

*I write this at the desk by the window, the workshop still behind me. The gears have stopped turning for the evening, and for once the silence is not a problem to be solved.*

Madonna Tassini,

You keep the shipping schedules. I keep the mechanisms. We have never spoken, which is itself an inefficiency worth correcting.

Here is my proposition, stated plainly because I have no talent for the other kind of speech: you observe the docks and know which cargo moves, when, and at whose cost. I build the machines that would sort it faster. Neither knowledge is worth much alone. The schedule without the mechanism is only a record of delay; the mechanism without the schedule is a solution looking for its problem.

I am told my reputation precedes me, and not kindly — that I count displaced weavers in ducats rather than in hands. The charge is fair. I mention it so you need not wonder whether I know.

If a conversation would serve your position, leave word. If not, this letter costs you nothing but the reading.

— Niccolò Barozzi
*Machina Rationis Vincit*
